Nucleotide vs. base

A nucleotide is a nitrogenous base + deoxyribose sugar + phosphate. A nucleoside is base + sugar. Each selectable residue here is a whole nucleotide; the coloured slab is its base. Formulas shown are for the free base.

Model, assumptions and sources
  • Schematic B-DNA, not atomically resolved. Spheres, slabs and tubes are simplified; the connectors are not exact bonds.
  • 1 scene unit = 1 Å. Rise 3.4 Å/bp, 10.5 bp/turn (twist 34.29°), so one turn = 35.7 Å. Backbone radius 10 Å; strands offset by 150°.
  • Sequence is illustrative, not from a real gene. Strand A runs 5′→3′ upward; strand B is its reverse complement and runs 5′→3′ downward.
  • Selection rule: keep the current residue if its base matches, otherwise take the nearest instance to the camera focus.
  • Compare with real B-DNA, e.g. PDB 1BNA, and NCBI Bookshelf.
